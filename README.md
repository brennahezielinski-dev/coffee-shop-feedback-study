# Three Sparrows Customer Feedback Analysis

> **Turning 100 customer comments into actionable insights using Excel, AI, and project-management thinking.**

## Overview

This project analyzes 100 fictional customer comments from Three Sparrows, a multi-location coffee shop, to identify recurring customer pain points, prioritize issues, and translate qualitative feedback into actionable recommendations.

The goal wasn't simply to summarize what customers said, but to ask:

**What is happening? Why does it matter? What should the business do next?**

## What I Analyzed

The dataset covers:

- 100 customer comments
- 4 locations
- 5 feedback channels
- Customer ratings and sentiment
- Product and service issues
- Ordering and mobile-app experiences
- Staff interactions
- Customer loyalty feedback

The overall average rating was **2.69/5**, with 53 negative, 21 neutral, and 26 positive comments.

## Key Findings

### 1. Execution is a bigger problem than the menu

Customer feedback suggests that the core drink recipes aren't necessarily the issue. Inconsistent preparation, burnt espresso, and syrup inconsistencies were stronger contributors to negative experiences.

### 2. Speed and ordering are major pain points

Wait times and mobile-app problems were consistently associated with low ratings. Customers reported long waits, inaccurate order-status updates, duplicate charges, missing rewards, and ignored modification requests.

### 3. Safety issues require immediate escalation

Although allergen and cleanliness complaints were smaller in volume, their potential consequences were significantly more serious. These issues were treated as escalation points rather than simply another category to rank by volume.

## From Feedback → Action

The analysis translated customer comments into three primary solution areas:

1. **Standardize drink preparation**
   - Calibrated syrup pumps
   - Espresso timing and grinder checks
   - Per-shift quality checklist

2. **Improve speed and order flow**
   - Better rush-hour staffing
   - Dedicated frappe workflow
   - Drive-thru time targets
   - Mobile-app issue resolution

3. **Improve customization and safety**
   - Sweetness options
   - Sugar-free flavor options
   - Clear allergen labeling
   - Dedicated equipment protocols for allergen-sensitive orders

## Project Management Thinking

This project demonstrates how I approach unstructured information:

**Collect → Categorize → Analyze → Prioritize → Escalate → Recommend → Monitor**

Rather than treating every customer complaint equally, I considered factors such as:

- Frequency
- Severity
- Customer impact
- Operational impact
- Risk
- Potential business consequences

## Tools

- **Microsoft Excel** — data organization and analysis
- **AI** — assisted analysis and synthesis
- **PDF/Document reporting** — communicating findings and recommendations

## Deliverables

📊 **Excel analysis**  
Raw feedback, categorization, ratings, and analysis.

📄 **Customer Feedback Analysis Report**  
A concise summary of findings, priorities, recommended actions, and caveats.

## What I Learned

The biggest takeaway was that good analysis isn't just about finding the most common problem.

A lower-volume issue can deserve immediate attention when the **severity and potential impact are much higher**.

This project helped me practice turning unstructured customer feedback into information that can support better operational decisions.
